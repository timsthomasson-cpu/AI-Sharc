# Template 1 Crosswalk — ADOPTED

**Status: Adopted by Tim on 30 Oct 2026.** This is the corrected crosswalk presented on 30 Oct 2026, merging the official Texas licence verification with the Google GCID / Yelp category check from the Places spreadsheet delivered 29 Oct 2026.

**Scope:** Template 1, Licensed home trades, four trades only: HVAC, Plumbing, Electrical, Roofing. Pest control, garage door, locksmith, appliance repair, and foundation repair were named in Template 1 earlier but were NOT checked and are NOT included here.

**What carries across all four trades, unchanged:** the AC Critical gates — consistent exact business name everywhere; installation/replacement AND repair in plain words, as separate services; explicit town + ZIP service area; one unique primary phone + working booking/quote route; readable hours or a true 24/7 claim; verifiable listing outside the website. Only the service vocabulary and the licence check change per trade.

## Crosswalk

| Trade | Google Primary Business Category (GCID) | Yelp category (alias) | NAICS — 2017 vs 2022 | Texas licence — audit wording |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HVAC | HVAC Contractor — gcid:hvac_contractor | Heating & Air Conditioning/HVAC — hvac | 2017: 238220. 2022: 23822 Plumbing and HVAC contractors (238221 residential / 238222 nonresidential) | TDLR TACLA/TACLB number published. Source form consistent with Texas Administrative Code ch. 75, §75.22. |
| Plumbing | Plumber — gcid:plumber | Plumbing — plumbing | 2017: 238220 — same code as HVAC. 2022: 23822 — same code as HVAC. Do NOT use NAICS alone to identify a firm; use the places Primary Business Category. | TSBPE, NOT TDLR. Check: "named Responsible Master Plumber (RMP) + Master Plumber licence number (begins M) + Certificate of Insurance on file, verifiable in TSBPE search." A Texas plumbing business itself holds no TSBPE credential; it must secure at least one RMP, who must maintain at least $300,000 general commercial liability insurance and may be RMP of record for only one company at a time. Sources: https://tsbpe.texas.gov/license-types/responsible-master-plumber/ and https://tsbpe.texas.gov/consumer-information/ |
| Electrical | Electrician — gcid:electrician | Electricians — electricians | 2017: 238210. 2022: 23821 Electrical and wiring contractors (238211 residential / 238212 nonresidential) | TDLR. Check: "TECL + named Master Electrician of Record." An Electrical Contractor licence number is preceded by "TECL", and the business must employ a licensed Master Electrician as Master Electrician of Record (or hold one itself). Do NOT check TECL alone, and do NOT check a Master credential alone. Nuance: a Master Electrician may only be assigned to a single Electrical Contractor unless they own more than 50% of the contracting business — do not write the single-assignment rule as absolute. Source: http://www.tdlr.texas.gov/electricians/apply/businesses/contractor-elec.htm |
| Roofing | Roofing Contractor — gcid:roofing_contractor | Roofing — roofing | 2017: 238160. 2022: 23816 Roofing contractors (238161 residential / 238162 nonresidential) | NO Texas state roofing licence — TDLR does not regulate roofing contractors. Do NOT use "publish licence number" as a Critical gate for roofing. Swap, for roofing only, to High Priority ranking items (not Critical): voluntary RCAT credential, manufacturer certification, city registration / permit compliance for the town served, and insurance. HB 3344 (89th Legislature, 2025) would have created TDLR reroofing-contractor licensing but is listed as Dead on 2 June 2025, and RCAT's 10 June 2025 letter says it died in the House Calendars committee. Do not rely on a commercial page claiming HB 3344 became law. Sources: https://www.billtrack50.com/billdetail/1845831 and https://web.rcat.net/external/wcpages/wcnews/newsarticledisplay.aspx?articleid=86 |

All four Google categories are in the Construction & Trades bucket in the Places spreadsheet delivered 29 Oct 2026. All four Yelp categories are available in the US.

## Caveats — still attached after adoption

- The Google list is the lobstr GitHub compilation, NOT an official Google-published list. GCIDs upgrade the names from secondary-guide agreement to a compiled list, but they do not make them officially verified.
- Meta / Facebook category names ("Heating, Ventilating & Air Conditioning Service", "Plumbing Service", "Electrician") are NOT verified — no downloadable Meta list was found and none was invented.
- Safe pre-pitch check, unchanged: confirm any category in the platform's own picker for a real profile before use in a pitch.
- NAICS version: check which version a firm or platform uses before quoting a code. The 2017 codes (238220, 238210, 238160) do not appear in the NAICS 2022 file; the 2022 equivalents above apply.
- No firm has been contacted or scored under this crosswalk by adoption alone. Adoption is a method decision, not evidence about any firm.
