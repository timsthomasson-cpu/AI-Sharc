A Category is not itself Pass or Fail, and there is no all-or-nothing Category point and no partial credit inside an Item. Do not confuse a Report Card Category with a Primary Business Category. | Category |
| Item | Item | A single check inside a Report Card Category, for example C4.2 diagnostic fee in dollars, or G1 exact name. There are 21 scored Items under Specification v2.0. An Item result is Pass, Missing, Conflict, or Not accessible (see Status value); on a card a Not accessible Item is shown as Fail — Not confirmed. C3.2 BBB accreditation and rating consistent with bbb.org and C4.1 six-line warranty card are Ranking Suggestions, not scored Items. | Check |
