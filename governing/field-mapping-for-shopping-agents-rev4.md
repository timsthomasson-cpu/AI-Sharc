| Google Business Profile categories (GCIDs) | Primary Business Category and bucket, e.g. hvac\_contractor in Construction & Trades | List used is the lobstr GitHub compilation, NOT an official Google-published list. Confirm in the GBP category picker before a pitch. |
| Yelp categories (aliases) | Primary Business Category alias, e.g. hvac, plumbing, electricians, roofing, available in US | From Yelp Developer Categories page; original category.json is gone (404). Confirm in Yelp picker before a pitch. |
| Meta / Facebook categories | Primary Business Category name as shown on a Meta place | No downloadable Meta list found; names in the crosswalk are NOT verified. Confirm in Meta picker before a pitch; do not invent a list. |
| NAICS 2022 (BLS QCEW industry titles) | Government industry classification, 2- to 6-digit hierarchy | One code can cover multiple Trades (23822 covers HVAC and plumbing). Never use NAICS alone to identify a firm. Check version before quoting a code; 2017 codes do not appear in NAICS 2022. |
| Texas TDLR (HVAC, Electrical) | Licence currency and holder for TACLA/TACLB and TECL | TDLR – All Licenses dataset on data.texas.gov (7358-krk7) verified 6 Nov 2026 as a bulk licence check: licence type, number, business name, county, expiration date, owner name, subtype; no status column — active means expiration on or after the audit date; licence\_number is numeric only (strip TACLA/TACLB/TECL prefix and leading zeros; match licence\_type + number together). Dataset update date must be recorded per audit; daily cadence not proven. |
| Texas TSBPE (Plumbing) | RMP licence, company, and COI insurer/expiry via daily RMP CSV | CSV carries INS\_EXPIRY\_DTE and INSURANCE\_COMPANY, but no $300,000 amount field. A past INS\_EXPIRY\_DTE on a Current licence needs Public License Search cross-check before failing the COI Item (see the TSBPE COI hard rule on the Trade Variations sheet). |
| BBB (bbb.org) | Accreditation and rating as shown on bbb.org itself | No public BBB API found in the searches to date. Browser/manual reading in v1, with no firm-supplied Pass; an unconfirmed BBB Item stays Not confirmed. An own-site BBB claim alone is not verification. |
| schema.org LocalBusiness/HVACBusiness JSON-LD (C7.3 LocalBusiness structured data) | Structured data for LocalBusiness, Service, opening hours, telephone, area served, aggregate rating, where a firm publishes it | The LocalBusiness structured data Item requires valid LocalBusiness (or HVACBusiness) JSON-LD on the home page and the town service page, naming the firm, unique primary phone, address, area served, and opening hours, matching the visible page. Schema that contradicts the visible page is Fail, not Pass. |
