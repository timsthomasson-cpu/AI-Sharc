Why AI agents matter for a hvac contractor

HVAC is the highest-stakes emergency hire in the home. A homeowner with a dead compressor asks their AI assistant who to call — and the assistant can only name firms whose sites it can find, read and trust. If your maintenance plans, emergency service and licence are buried in images or PDFs, the machines skip you.

What the audit looks at for hvac contractors

Every trade gets the same honest, sourced audit. What counts as "findable, readable and trustworthy" differs by trade, so the audit pays attention to what your customers and the machines both check first.

Emergency and service clarity in text

Can a machine tell, in plain text, that you answer emergency calls and what your hours are?

Maintenance plans as offerings

Agents recommend firms with recurring-revenue plans. If your plan isn't described in text, it doesn't exist to them.

Your state licence signals

Your TACLIC/contractor licence details stated clearly, where homeowners and machines both look.

Service area in words

The towns and counties you serve, written out — not just a map graphic.
