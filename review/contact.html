About AI Sharc

We started AI Sharc because the way homeowners find a plumber, an HVAC tech or a roofer is changing: they ask AI assistants, not search engines. A business that the machines can't find, read and trust is invisible to those customers — no matter how good the work is.

So we built the audit we wished existed: honest, sourced, graded A–F, with bite-sized fixes. We're based in Coppell, Texas, and we launched with Texas licensed home trades — the businesses where trust decides the hire.

Bite-sized fixes. Shark-sized results.
