NOTES AND LIMITS

This report card does not guarantee inclusion or a ranking in any AI recommendation. It only measures AI Shopping Health on the evidence checked, for the query, town, and ZIP above, on the date performed.

High Priority counts are issue counts, not fix-list row counts: one issue per row, so a row bundling two issues counts as two.

To prove improvement, re-run the same shopping query, same category, same town and ZIP, on a new date, and record the new approximate ranking. If the category score improves but the approximate ranking does not, the report card must be recalibrated for that market.

How pass and fail are decided (hard rules, version 2026-11-19): An item passes only if it meets its stated criteria exactly, with the source and date shown; there is no partial credit and no ‘close enough’. Each item is Pass or Fail and worth the points shown; a passed item earns its full points, a failed item earns 0. A category’s score is the sum of the points of the items in it that passed. The total is out of 100. Pass with warning counts as a pass: the item meets its criteria, but a note is shown with it, for example a rating that is readable with its platform, review count, and date, but differs across platforms. Ratings from different platforms are expected to differ, so they are reported per platform, never averaged, and a cross-platform difference on its own is not treated as a conflict. Grade F is given only when the website is unreachable on the firm’s side. A closed firm is not audited and receives no Report Card. The eight ranking suggestions — C3.2 BBB accreditation and rating consistent with bbb.org and C4.1 six-line warranty card, and the six Section 6 factors (S1 Booking and quote route, S2 Availability, S3 Price for this user, S4 Brand and system coverage, S5 Service-area dispatch, and S6 Machine discovery (llms.txt)) — are recommendations, not scored, and never change your grade. A ranking suggestion is shown as observed only where public evidence directly proves it; otherwise it is shown as a recommendation.
