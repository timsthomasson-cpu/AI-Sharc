Most contractors are invisible to AI

Ask an AI assistant for an HVAC company in almost any town and you'll get the same handful of names — the big brands, the aggregators, the firms with machine-readable websites. Most independent contractors aren't in the answer at all.

The estimate

Industry analysis in 2026 estimated that the large majority of independent HVAC and plumbing firms have effectively zero presence in AI answers for their own city and service. Treat the exact figure with caution — but the pattern is easy to verify: ask an AI for a contractor in your town and see who comes back. (Source: industry analysis, 2026.)

Why the big names dominate

National brands and lead aggregators have structured, text-first websites: every service named, every city listed, every trust signal in machine-readable form. AI assistants favour what they can read and verify. That's not an unfair advantage — it's a buildable one.

Invisibility is usually self-inflicted

The most common causes are mundane: services described only in photos, a service area that lives inside a map graphic, a phone number that isn't tappable text, licence and insurance details locked in a PDF. Each one is fixable in an afternoon.

The takeaway

The window is open. Most of your competitors haven't done the work, which makes every fix disproportionately valuable — for now.
