Google now books services inside the search page

The machine-shopping era stopped being theoretical. Google's AI Mode now supports agentic booking for local experiences and services, and agentic checkout lets shoppers complete purchases without visiting the seller's site.

What shipped

Google rolled out agentic capabilities in Search — including booking flows for local services powered by its Universal Commerce Protocol — and agentic checkout that completes purchases inside AI Mode and Gemini. (Sources: Google announcements, 2025–2026; industry coverage.)

Why this matters to a home-trade firm

The assistant that books the job is the assistant that was trusted to recommend it. Booking flows need machine-readable services, availability and contact details — the same raw material as recommendations, one step further.

The pattern to watch

Every platform is converging on the same requirement: businesses whose facts are structured and readable get recommended, cited and transacted with. Businesses whose facts aren't, don't.

The takeaway

The work you do to be recommendable today is the same work that makes you bookable tomorrow. It compounds.
