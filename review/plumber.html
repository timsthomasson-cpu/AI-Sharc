Why AI agents matter for a plumber

Plumbing searches are urgent and local, which makes them exactly the kind of question people now hand to an AI assistant. The agent builds its shortlist from what it can read about you: your emergency availability, your licence, your service area. If those live only in photos or a phone number in a footer image, you're not on the list.
