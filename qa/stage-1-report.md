calls. Palette: navy `#123B63`, steel `#2E6FA3`, teal `#00D4C8`.

## Exposure check (rule 1 — Audit-Spec v1.21 §12)

The how-it-works page describes the audit in marketing terms only: the three find/read/trust questions,
the A–F grade, free Preliminary vs paid Final, sourced findings (page + wording + date), no partial
credit, and an explicit line that the detailed scoring is not published. **No scoring details, item
lists, weights, thresholds, category names, or Critical Gates content appear on any public page.**
Automated leak scan (qa/qa_check.py, ~20 leak phrases) passed on all 13 pages. Sample cards are
dummy-data Preliminary cards showing grade + summary findings only.
