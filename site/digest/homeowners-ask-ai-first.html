A third of homeowners now ask AI who to hire

When a water heater dies at 9pm, a growing share of homeowners no longer open Google. They ask an AI assistant who to call — and the assistant names two or three firms. If yours isn't named, the job isn't yours.

The number

A 2026 Scorpion national study found that 22% of homeowners go to ChatGPT first, not Google, when they need a contractor. Broader 2026 surveys put AI-first contractor search between 22% and 45% depending on age group. Either way: the front door to your business is no longer only a search box. (Source: Scorpion national study, 2026; industry surveys, 2026.)

Why this hits home trades first

Home services are exactly the kind of purchase AI assistants handle well: local, urgent, trust-heavy. The assistant already knows the customer's town from context. It shortlists firms it can find, read and trust — then hands over a phone number.

What decides who gets named

The assistant can only recommend what it can read. Firms whose services, service area, licence and proof are stated in plain text on their site get shortlisted. Firms whose information lives in images, PDFs or nowhere get skipped — no matter how good they are.

The takeaway

This isn't a future trend to watch. It's how a meaningful share of your customers already behave. The question isn't whether AI assistants recommend home-trade firms — it's whether they can recommend you.
