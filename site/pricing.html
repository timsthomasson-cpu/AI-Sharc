What you're paying for — and what you're not

You are paying for

  • An honest, sourced, graded read on how AI agents see your site.
  • A fix list you can act on without hiring anyone.
  • Optional monitoring so you know the fixes worked.

You are not paying for

  • Implementation. AI Sharc audits; it doesn't touch your site.
  • A plugin or software to install.
  • A contract. Everything after the Final card is month-to-month, cancelled by you in your dashboard.
