The service

AI Sharc audits business websites for AI-agent readiness and issues graded Report Cards (A–F). The Preliminary Report Card is free. The Final Report Card is a paid, one-time purchase. Monitoring is a month-to-month subscription you may cancel at any time.

What the audit is — and isn't

The audit assesses how easily automated AI shopping agents can find, read and trust information on your website, based on the publicly visible content of that site on the date audited. It is not a guarantee of traffic, rankings, revenue or AI-assistant recommendations. Grades describe your site as checked; sites change, and grades reflect the date of the audit.

Every finding is sourced

Findings cite the checked page, its exact wording and the check date so you can verify them. If you believe a finding is wrong (for example, the page changed after we checked), contact us and we'll re-check.

Your responsibilities

Submit only websites you own or are authorised to represent. Provide a valid business email for verification. Don't submit sites to probe, stress or abuse the service.

Payments and cancellation

The Final Report Card is a one-time payment ($99 introductory price for the first 500 firms; regular price $149). Your first months of monitoring are included with the Final Report Card; after the included months, monitoring continues at $25/month introductory (regular $30/month), billed monthly, cancellable by you at any time from your dashboard. Cancellation stops future billing; the current month is not refunded.

No professional advice

Report Cards and fixes are marketing and technical guidance for your website. They are not legal, insurance, licensing or tax advice.

Limitation of liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, AI Sharc's liability arising from the service is limited to the amounts you paid us in the 12 months before the claim.
