Why AI agents matter for a roofing contractor

Roofing is the biggest single hire a homeowner makes, and Texas doesn't license roofers at the state level — so an AI agent has no licence to lean on. It recommends the firm whose site proves, in readable text, that it's real: insurance, manufacturer certifications, local track record. If that proof is missing or locked in images, the machines can't tell you apart from a storm-chaser.

What the audit looks at for roofing contractors

Every trade gets the same honest, sourced audit. What counts as "findable, readable and trustworthy" differs by trade, so the audit pays attention to what your customers and the machines both check first.

Proof instead of licence

Insurance, manufacturer certifications (GAF, Owens Corning and similar) and warranties stated in text — the signals that stand in for a state licence.

Storm and insurance-claim clarity

Can a machine tell that you handle insurance claims, and how the process works for the homeowner?

Local track record in words

Towns served and years in the area written as text an agent can cite.

Material and service specifics

Replacement, repair, inspection and materials named so agents match the job to you.
