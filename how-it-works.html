How the audit works

AI Sharc checks your website the way AI shopping agents do: can they find you, read you, and trust you enough to recommend you? Then you get a graded Report Card with the fixes.

Three questions, one grade

An AI shopping agent — ChatGPT, Gemini, a voice assistant — only recommends businesses it can deal with. Our audit asks the same three questions it asks, in the same order:

Can they find you?

When someone asks an AI for your service in your area, can the agent even tell that you exist and serve that area?

Can they read you?

Once it lands on your site, can the machine actually read what you do, where you do it, and how to reach you? Or is that information locked in images and forms?

Can they trust you?

Does your site give an agent reasons to recommend you over the next firm — the proof, the specifics and the signals that separate real businesses from thin ones?

A graded report card, not a wall of tech talk

The grade

Your site gets one overall letter grade from A to F, the way a report card works. No partial credit and no averaging: what fails, fails, and you see exactly what it is.

The findings

Every finding is a specific, plain-English statement about your site — and each one cites its source: the page we checked, the exact wording, and the date. Nothing on your card is our opinion of your industry. It's what's on your site.

What we check

In plain terms, we check everything that decides whether an AI agent can find, read and trust your business: how your site describes your services and your service area, whether the essentials (contact, hours, licence, proof) are stated in text a machine can read, how your site holds up against the basics agents rely on, and whether the signals that build trust are present and consistent.

We don't publish the detailed scoring behind the grade — it's what keeps the audit honest and hard to game. Your own card tells you everything about your site.
